- The assignment requires at least five real prompt/output correction examples. Three conversational examples are recorded in [AI evidence](./ai-evidence.md); two more must be captured from actual reviewed interactions. The repository owner should confirm whether the rubric accepts conversational assistant examples for this item.

The explicit queue skip is covered by a synthetic-page test and was not triggered in the latest run. The smoke subset passed three consecutive times.
- The tracked RCA summary now reflects the latest 40/40 run with zero ordinary failures and two controlled-fault records. The earlier AE-12 queue-full incident is documented separately; the collector detects that signal in future failure bundles. Both controlled classifications are approved, but no production patch was validated.
- Prior GitHub retry causes are recorded above. The workflow now fails after artifact upload when a test passes only after a retry; the current PR and manual runs verified the gate with no flaky tests.
- The rubric requires at least 85/100, RCA at least 14/18, and AI-agent design at least 11/15 for READY. This estimate reaches the AI-design gate but remains below the total and RCA gates.
- Do not claim Level-3 readiness until the RCA subscore gate, remaining prompt evidence, validated patch (if a product defect is found), and required CI evidence are complete.
