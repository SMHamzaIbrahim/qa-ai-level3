Four conversational examples and one model-free RCA review decision are documented in [AI evidence](./ai-evidence.md); evaluator acceptance of the fifth candidate is uncertain. The explicit queue skip is covered by a synthetic-page test and was not triggered in the latest run. The smoke subset passed three consecutive times.

PR run #20 is documented separately: the owner approved the 10 explicit HTTP 403 response cases as third-party response failures, while 17 UI timeouts remain pending. Main run #21 passed 40/40 afterward; that recovery does not prove the cause of run #20.

- The rubric requires at least 85/100, RCA at least 14/18, and AI-agent design at least 11/15 for READY. This provisional estimate now meets those numeric gates; it is not an evaluator award.
- Do not claim Level-3 readiness until the live defense and qualifying prompt/correction evidence are complete, and any additional evaluator-required evidence is available.
